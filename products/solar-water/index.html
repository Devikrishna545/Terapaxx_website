Solar Water Heaters

Terrapaxx solar water heaters deliver dependable hot water with solar-only operation, lower running cost, and scalable capacity for domestic to industrial deployments.

Why Solar Water Heating?

Eliminate recurring water-heating energy cost. Systems run on solar energy and store hot water for reliable day and night access.

Solutions For Every Scale

  • Domestic: Daily hot water for homes, apartments and villas with low maintenance.
  • Commercial & Industrial: Ideal for hotels, hospitals, hostels, factories and process heating.
  • Capacity: 100 litres onwards, designed to exact usage requirements.

Key Benefits

  • Zero operating cost with pure solar heating
  • ETC collectors provide strong low-light and cold-weather performance
  • Food-grade tanks for safe and hygienic hot-water storage
  • High insulation for overnight heat retention
  • Corrosion-resistant build for tropical and coastal conditions
  • Scalable modular design for future expansion

Why ETC Over Flat Plate?

ETC tubes use vacuum insulation around each collector, reducing heat loss and sustaining higher thermal output even on overcast days. This improves season-long performance and savings.
