Our product line covers complete energy needs from generation and storage to heating and backup reliability. Below is a concise overview of our major offerings.

Solar Power Systems

Comprehensive SPV topologies from 500W to 100KW+ including standalone backup and grid-connected net-metering systems.

  • Polycrystalline modules with strong low-light performance (250W to 360W)
  • Inverter range from 1000 VA to 25 KVA with MPPT/PWM options
  • Single/three phase outputs with pure sine wave and protection features

Solar Water Heaters

Efficient and cost-effective hot water systems for domestic and industrial use with capacities starting from 100 litres.

  • ETC collector technology for reliable thermal capture
  • Stainless steel and polymer tank options
  • High corrosion resistance and good heat retention

Home UPS / Inverters

Reliable home and commercial backup solutions designed for stable output and intelligent protection during outages.

  • Sine wave inverter architecture with advanced digital controls
  • Wide input voltage acceptance and LCD parameter display
  • Compatible with flat plate, tubular and VRLA batteries

Inverter Batteries

Durable power storage batteries for inverter, UPS and solar applications across homes and offices.

  • Robust tubular plate technology
  • Deep cycling performance for dependable backup
  • Low maintenance with extended service life
