Inverter Batteries

Detailed

The Power Behind Your Power

A backup system is only as reliable as the battery running it. Terrapaxx inverter batteries are built for deep, repeated cycling and long-lasting performance for homes, offices, and solar installations.

Tubular Technology - Built to Last

Tubular plate construction handles frequent deep discharge cycles with significantly better real-world longevity than conventional flat plate batteries.

  • Ideal for: regions with frequent or prolonged power cuts
  • Ideal for: solar energy storage applications
  • Ideal for: homes and offices with high daily backup demand

Key Benefits

Feature Advantage
Tubular Plate DesignHigher energy density and longer cycle life
Deep Cycle CapabilityReliable repeated charge-discharge performance
Low MaintenanceLess topping-up and reduced downtime
Long Service LifeLower total ownership cost over time
Solar CompatibleEngineered for inverter and SPV systems

Why Tubular Over Flat Plate?

Flat plate batteries work, but tubular batteries endure. The tubular spine design resists active material shedding, resulting in more usable cycles, longer life, and better value over time.

Available in multiple capacities to match inverter size and backup duration. Pairs with Terrapaxx inverters and solar PCUs for optimized performance.
